# Celosphere 2025 — Product News (Celonis)

Source: Celonis public blog — "Celosphere 2025: Everything you need to know"

## Overview
Celosphere is the annual Celonis user conference, held in Munich. The 2025 edition centered on a single theme: making Enterprise AI actually work. Celonis reaffirmed its mission — to make processes work for people, companies, and the planet — and announced platform updates aimed at building what it calls the AI-driven, composable enterprise: a business that improves continuously by using AI to automate and enhance daily operations.

## Opening message
Co-founder and co-CEO Alexander Rinke opened the conference by calling on attendees to embrace the next transformation: making Enterprise AI truly work, rather than leaving AI stuck in isolated pilots.

## Major product announcements

### Celonis Data Core
The platform's high-performance data infrastructure reached general availability. Data Core makes it easier to get data into Celonis and to process and query that data at high speed and scale. It is positioned as the foundation that feeds the rest of the platform.

### Process Intelligence Graph (PI Graph)
The PI Graph received updates as the central knowledge layer connecting and interpreting process data across enterprise systems. It is positioned as the context layer that makes enterprise AI effective.

### Orchestration Engine
Celonis announced the Orchestration Engine, combining the Process Intelligence Graph with real-time autonomous execution. Celonis frames the combination of insight plus orchestration as a step toward the "autonomous enterprise."

## Customer proof points announced
- Mercedes-Benz uses Celonis to optimize production across more than 30 factories worldwide, connecting data into its MO360 platform, with AI co-pilots predicting delivery times and optimizing planning.
- Vinmar collaborates with Celonis to streamline global supply chains using AI and Process Intelligence.
- Florida Crystals Corporation expanded its agreement with Celonis.
- Celonis announced a partnership with Databricks to power enterprise AI that continuously improves operations.

## Core narrative
The recurring message at Celosphere 2025: Enterprise AI only works when it is connected to process context. Companies that combine Process Intelligence with AI tools report measurable improvements in efficiency, quality, and decision-making. Without operational context, AI struggles because the underlying processes are not well understood.
