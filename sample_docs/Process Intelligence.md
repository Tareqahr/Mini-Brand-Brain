# What is Process Intelligence (Celonis)

Source: Celonis public blog — "What is Process Intelligence and how will it help you optimize your business processes"

## The core problem
Every enterprise runs on processes that connect departments, systems, and people, and these processes determine how well the business delivers value. In practice, processes rarely run the way they were designed. Teams design a clean linear process expecting a few variants, but real execution quickly turns into a tangled "spaghetti" of paths. Celonis cites its Process Optimization Report finding that 63% of enterprise leaders say sub-optimal processes cost time and reduce productivity, even though 83% see processes as their greatest lever for value and fastest lever for change.

## What Process Intelligence is
Celonis describes Process Intelligence as the connective tissue between an enterprise's people, systems, and processes. It gives the whole business a shared understanding of how processes actually work, how they interact, and how to improve them. It starts by pulling data from systems like ERPs, CRMs, and Excel, then uses process mining technology to turn that data into a living, moving digital twin of the company's end-to-end processes. AI algorithms combined with process improvement expertise then surface where value is hidden, and the platform provides tools to analyze, improve, and monitor those processes.

## Seven ways Process Intelligence optimizes processes

1. Visibility and data-driven decisions — standardizes process data in one platform, removing guesswork and gut feeling from decision-making.

2. Uncovering value — analytics surface value opportunities and inefficiencies, from bottlenecks to redesign opportunities. Celonis cites Deutsche Telekom Services Europe saving over €66M in procure-to-pay.

3. Targeted automation — identifies repetitive high-volume manual tasks suited to RPA, and can simulate the cross-business impact of an automation before implementation.

4. An enablement layer for AI — gives AI and ML algorithms a data-informed understanding of how the organization's processes work and interact, so AI can model the impact of process changes and respond to anomalies.

5. Performance monitoring and continuous improvement — dashboards track process performance against business objectives, including early-warning alerts when performance drops out of range. Celonis cites PepsiCo reducing rejected sales orders by 86%.

6. Team collaboration — a shared visualization lets different business functions work together on process discovery, breaking down silos.

7. Compliance and risk — a digital twin of real processes lets leaders spot anomalies, non-compliance, and adoption rates. Celonis cites Cardinal Health achieving 60% growth in user adoption over 12 months.

## Key distinction
Celonis positions Process Intelligence as distinct from Business Intelligence. BI analyzes historical data; Process Intelligence reveals how processes actually run in real time and enables action on those insights.
