# Celonis Brand FAQs

## Product Positioning

### How should we describe what Celonis does in one sentence?
Celonis is the Process Intelligence platform that helps enterprises understand how their operations actually run and take immediate action to improve them.

### Can we still say "process mining"?
Process mining is the technology Celonis invented and still uses. However, it should not be used as the primary product descriptor in external communications. Celonis is a Process Intelligence platform. Process mining is part of how we deliver that intelligence — not the headline of what we are. Use "process mining" only when giving historical context or explaining the underlying technology to a technical audience.

### Is Celonis an AI company?
Celonis is a Process Intelligence company that uses AI extensively throughout its platform. We do not lead with "AI company" as a primary descriptor — it is too generic and does not communicate our category. Always ground AI claims in specific capabilities. Say "AI that identifies and predicts bottlenecks in your order-to-cash process" rather than "AI-powered platform."

### How is Celonis different from a BI tool?
Business intelligence tools help you analyze historical data. Celonis shows you how your processes are actually running in real time — including deviations, exceptions, and root causes — and enables you to act on those insights inside your existing systems. BI tells you what happened. Celonis tells you why and what to do about it.

### Does Celonis replace SAP or other ERPs?
No. Celonis connects to and enhances your existing enterprise systems — SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, ServiceNow, and more. It sits above your systems as an intelligence and action layer. It makes your existing investments more valuable, not obsolete.

## Tone and Language

### What capitalization rules apply to our key terms?
Always capitalize: Process Intelligence, Process Intelligence Graph. Do not capitalize: process mining (when used as a technical descriptor), digital twin (unless part of a specific named product).

### How do we talk about competitors?
We do not name competitors in external marketing materials. We describe our category clearly and let the differentiation speak for itself. If a customer or journalist asks directly, acknowledge the competitive landscape briefly and redirect to what makes Celonis distinct.

### What is the elevator pitch for a CFO?
Celonis shows you exactly where money is being lost in your processes — delayed invoices, excess inventory, missed early payment discounts, inefficient procurement cycles — and helps you recover it. Most customers see measurable financial impact within their first quarter.

### What should we say about implementation time?
Celonis connects to enterprise systems in weeks. Avoid committing to specific timelines in marketing content that could become contractual. Use "fast time to value" and "weeks, not months" as general guidance. Let sales and solutions teams qualify specific timelines.

## Events and Campaigns

### How formal should event communications be?
Match the formality of the audience. C-suite executive roundtables: formal, evidence-led, brief. User conferences and community events: warmer, more conversational. Field events and webinars: professional but approachable. When in doubt, lean slightly more formal — it is easier to warm up a formal tone than to correct an overly casual one.

### Can we use customer quotes before they are approved?
No. All customer quotes, case study content, and attributed statistics must be approved in writing by the customer before use. Route all requests through the customer advocacy team.
