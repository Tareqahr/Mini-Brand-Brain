# Celonis Messaging Framework

## Core Message

Celonis is the Process Intelligence platform. We help the world's largest enterprises understand how their operations actually run — and take immediate action to improve them.

## Primary Value Proposition

Celonis connects to your existing enterprise systems, extracts process data in real time, and surfaces where value is being lost. Then it helps you act on those insights — automatically, at scale, inside the systems your teams already use.

## Audience-Specific Messages

### For the C-Suite
Celonis turns operational data into financial outcomes. We show exactly where cash flow is constrained, where working capital is trapped, and where process inefficiencies are costing money — then help you recover it.

### For Operations Leaders
Celonis gives your team a real-time view of how every process is actually running across your enterprise. No more guesswork. No more spreadsheets. You see deviations the moment they happen and resolve them before they escalate.

### For IT and Technology Leaders
Celonis integrates with SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, ServiceNow, and hundreds of other enterprise systems without replacing them. Implementation is measured in weeks. Data stays in your environment.

## Messaging Guardrails

- Do not lead with "AI" — lead with Process Intelligence and ground any AI claim in a specific capability.
- Do not use "digital transformation" as a standalone phrase — it is too vague.
- Do not describe Celonis as a BI tool, analytics platform, or dashboard solution.
- Always connect features to business outcomes: cost savings, revenue acceleration, risk reduction.

## Proof Points to Use

- Celonis customers typically see measurable financial impact within the first quarter.
- The Celonis platform analyzes billions of process events across enterprise systems.
- Over 1,000 enterprise customers across manufacturing, retail, financial services, and more rely on Celonis.
