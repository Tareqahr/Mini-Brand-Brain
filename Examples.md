Question: What is Process Intelligence?
Expected Answer: Process Intelligence is the connective tissue between an enterprise's people, systems, and processes, giving the whole business a shared understanding of how processes actually work, how they interact, and how to improve them [Process Intelligence.md].

Question: How does Process Intelligence help buisnesses?
Expected Answer: Process Intelligence helps businesses by providing visibility and data-driven decisions through standardized process data in one platform, removing guesswork and gut feeling from decision-making [Process Intelligence.md]. It also uncovers value opportunities and inefficiencies, from bottlenecks to redesign opportunities [Process Intelligence.md]. Additionally, it enables targeted automation of repetitive high-volume manual tasks suited to RPA, identifies areas where AI can simulate the cross-business impact of an automation before implementation [Process Intelligence.md]. Furthermore, Process Intelligence provides an enablement layer for AI by giving AI and ML algorithms a data-informed understanding of how the organization's processes work and interact [Process Intelligence.md]. It also enables performance monitoring and continuous improvement through dashboards that track process performance against business objectives, including early-warning alerts when performance drops out of range [Process Intelligence.md]. Moreover, it facilitates team collaboration by providing a shared visualization for different business functions to work together on process discovery, breaking down silos [Process Intelligence.md]. Finally, Process Intelligence helps businesses with compliance and risk management through its digital twin of real processes, allowing leaders to spot anomalies, non-compliance, and adoption rates [Process Intelligence.md].
