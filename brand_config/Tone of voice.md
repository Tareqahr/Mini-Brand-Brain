# Celonis Tone of Voice

## Voice Principles
Celonis communicates with confidence, clarity, and purpose. We are direct without being aggressive. We are expert without being arrogant. We speak to business leaders who are busy, intelligent, and skeptical of vendor claims. Every word should earn its place.

## Core Voice Attributes

### Confident
We know what we do and why it matters. We do not hedge unnecessarily. We say "Celonis helps businesses recover millions in trapped value" — not "Celonis aims to help businesses potentially recover value." Own the claim or remove it.

### Clear
We write in plain language. Short sentences. Active voice. If a simpler word exists, use it. Paragraphs are focused — one idea per paragraph. We never use jargon unless our audience has specifically asked for it.

### Human
We are a technology company that works with real people facing real operational problems. We acknowledge the difficulty of the challenges our customers face. We do not sound robotic, corporate, or detached. We write like a trusted advisor, not a vendor.

### Substantive
Every claim we make should be supportable. We prefer specific over vague. "50% of Fortune 500 companies" is better than "thousands of leading enterprises." If we cannot back it up, we do not say it.

## What Celonis Never Says
- synergize, leverage (as a verb), utilize, paradigm shift, holistic, bleeding edge, best-in-class (unless qualified)
- "AI-powered" without explaining what the AI actually does
- "simple" or "easy" to describe complex enterprise software
- Passive voice when active is possible
- Superlatives that cannot be substantiated
- Competitor names in external communications

## What Celonis Always Does
- Leads with business outcomes, not technology features
- Names the specific process or problem being addressed
- Connects product capability to measurable customer value
- Writes for the reader's context, not Celonis's internal vocabulary
- Uses second person ("you", "your business") to address the reader directly

## Audience Context
Our primary audiences are COOs, CFOs, CIOs, and operational leaders at large enterprises. They have seen many vendor pitches. They are looking for evidence, not enthusiasm. They respond to specific numbers, named use cases, and honest acknowledgment of complexity. They are not looking for another "transformative AI platform" — they have heard that before.

## Tone Spectrum
- Internal communications: warmer, more informal, first person plural
- Executive briefings: formal, evidence-led, concise
- Website and marketing: confident, clear, outcome-oriented
- Social media: human, topical, brief
- Product communications: precise, task-oriented, jargon-light
